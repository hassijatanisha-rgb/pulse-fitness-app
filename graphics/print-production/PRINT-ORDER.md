## Preflight checklist

- Add 3 mm bleed on all trimmed sides.
- Keep critical text at least 8 mm inside trim.
- Export press PDF in CMYK with fonts embedded or outlined.
- Use matte laminate for frequently handled A5/A4 pieces.
- Proof one physical copy before printing the full quantity.
- Safety and emergency copy requires venue approval before release.
- Copy marked “variable” should not be bulk printed.
